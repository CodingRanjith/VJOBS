Our Services

Ten core overseas manpower and recruitment support services listed in the company brochure.

01

Recruitment

End-to-end overseas recruitment support covering requirement intake, candidate search, screening, shortlisting and coordination through to selection.

02

Visa Service

Support for visa-related processing steps for selected candidates as part of overseas deployment workflows.

03

Document Attestation

Assistance with document attestation requirements connected to foreign employment and embassy submissions.

04

Immigration (NON Clearance)

Support related to immigration formalities as listed in the brochure under Immigration (NON Clearance).

05

Orientation

Candidate orientation support to help selected manpower prepare for overseas employment transitions.

06

Client Interview

Coordination of final interviews by the employer or authorised representative at our facilities or designated locations.

07

Air Ticketing

Travel ticketing coordination for selected candidates departing to the destination country.

08

Manpower

Supply of manpower across unskilled, semi-skilled, skilled and professional categories for international employers.

09

Consultation

Advisory support for employers seeking guidance on overseas manpower planning and recruitment pathways.

10

FWCMS Medical

FWCMS Medical coordination as listed in the brochure, noted as approved by all medical centres.
