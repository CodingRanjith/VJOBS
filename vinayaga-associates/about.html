Company Overview

Who we are

Vinayaga Associates (Overseas Manpower Recruiting Agency) was incorporated on 28 February 2006 and is a premium manpower consultant service based in Chennai at #73/7, 1st Floor, Ethiraj Swamy Salai, Erukkancherry, Chennai – 600 118, Tamil Nadu, India (Near Pavithra Hospital).

We offer premier hiring solutions across banking, finance, insurance, construction, infrastructure, automobile, healthcare, education and more, supported by extensive databanks of unskilled, semi-skilled, skilled and professional candidates.
