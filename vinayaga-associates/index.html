About Vinayaga Associates

Premium overseas manpower consultancy from Chennai

Incorporated on 28 February 2006, Vinayaga Associates provides premier hiring solutions across banking, finance, insurance, construction, infrastructure, automobile, healthcare, education and related sectors.

Our extensive databanks cover unskilled, semi-skilled, skilled and professional candidates, supported by documentation, medical coordination and emigration formalities.
