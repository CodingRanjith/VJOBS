eMigrate / FE Process

Employer registration & demand documents

Registration themes

  • FE registration through the eMigrate portal
  • Upload of mandatory documents
  • Registration certificate
  • Letter of request
  • Passport copies of directors
  • ARN and PDF generation

Documents commonly listed for demand

  • SSM employer details
  • KDN quota approval
  • Power of Attorney
  • Employment contract
  • Letter of Undertaking
  • Demand letter

Attestation guidance

Documents are typically prepared with 2 or 3 sets of notary attestation — one copy for the Embassy and another for self-retention. After demand submission, a unique Demand ID and Job ID are created for the employer.

Medical & emigration formalities

  • Complete medical examination for selected candidates
  • FWCMS Medical coordination
  • Visa endorsement of selected candidates
  • Completion of emigration formalities
  • Departure coordination to destination
