Recruitment Procedure

How it works — from manpower requirements to departure, based on the company brochure workflow.

Process Timeline

Step-by-step recruitment flow

Regular contact and familiarity give an advantage in the job. We not only ensure the originality of certificates but also work to accomplish the assignment as early as possible.

Discuss Your Requirement

1. Receive manpower requirements

Action begins on receipt of requirements of manpower from our clients.

2. Search existing candidate database

We browse the existing databank for candidates who meet the precise job specification.

3. Identify suitable candidates

If suitable candidates are available, the process continues to screening.

4. Advertise and source when required

If suitable candidates are not available, vacancies are advertised in newspapers and sourced through private hunting channels.

5. Screen applications

A large number of applications are screened against role requirements.

6. Expert interviews and shortlisting

Interviews by experts are conducted to shortlist suitable candidates.

7. Practical trade tests

Practical / trade tests are arranged in workshop or site settings where required.

8. Final employer interview

Final interview by the employer or authorised representative.

9. Medical examination

Complete medical exam, including FWCMS Medical (approved by all medical centres as stated in the brochure).

10. Visa endorsement

Visa endorsements of the selected candidates.

11. Emigration formalities

Completion of the emigration formalities.

12. Departure coordination

Departure from the airport to the destination.

FE Registration

FE registration & process to raise demand

Overview adapted from the brochure flowchart referencing the eMigrate portal (www.emigrate.gov.in). This is informational content from company materials, not legal advice.

1

FE registration

Foreign Employer registration through the eMigrate portal.

2

Upload mandatory documents

Registration certificate, letter of request and passport copies of directors, among other required uploads.

3

ARN & PDF generation

ARN and PDF are generated after registration steps.

4

Document collection

Collect SSM employer details, KDN quota approval, Power of Attorney, Employment Contract, Letter of Undertaking and Demand Letter. Brochure guidance notes 2–3 sets with notary attestation.

5

Submit to Indian Mission

Submit documents to the Indian Mission Officer as applicable.

6

Raise demand via RA mode

Login with credentials to raise demand through Recruiting Agent (RA) mode, fill job details and upload employer approval copy.

Outcome noted in brochure

After submission, a unique Demand ID and Job ID are created for the employer. With the Job ID, the Recruiting Agent can submit emigrant documents to the Protector of Emigrants (POE) of India to obtain emigration clearance. Foreign employers can recruit Indian workers through Recruiting Agents of India.

Some brochure flowchart details (exact document numbering, browser version notes, and mission submission nuances) should be verified against current eMigrate / MEA guidance before presenting them as operational instructions.
